Photography direction: real editorial photography of owners, operators, boards, and
operating businesses. Avoid stock handshakes, generic skyscrapers, luxury imagery
(yachts/watches/jets/cars), posed teams staring at camera, or generic diverse-stock-photo
conference rooms. Crop editorially with natural proportions; keep text off busy areas.
